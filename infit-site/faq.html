Membership & Pricing
How much does a membership at inFIT24 cost?
inFIT24 memberships start at $55/month. Options include the Gym tier, Plus, Premium, Ultimate, and Family memberships. A $10 day pass, week pass, and month pass are also available for visitors.
What is included in the Gym membership?
The Gym membership includes 24/7 gym access, unlimited sauna sessions, a 20-minute equipment orientation with a personal trainer, one group exercise class, and one group training session to try. Additional classes and training sessions are available at a discounted member rate.
What is the difference between Plus, Premium, and Ultimate memberships?
All three include 24/7 gym and sauna access.

Plus — 4 group exercise classes per month
Premium — Unlimited group exercise classes
Ultimate — Unlimited group exercise classes + unlimited group training
What is the Family membership and who qualifies?
The Family Membership is designed for 3–5 household family members and includes 24/7 gym access, infrared sauna, and an equipment orientation, plus discounted group exercise classes and training. If you need to add just one family member to an existing membership, contact us about our family member add-on discount.
Does inFIT24 require a contract?
No. All inFIT24 memberships are month-to-month with no contracts. A 30-day notice is required to cancel.
How do I cancel my membership?
You can cancel directly from the Membership section in the inFIT247 app. A 30-day notice is required.
How do I sign up for a membership?
You can sign up three ways: stop in during staffed hours for a tour and sign up in person, sign up on our website, or download the inFIT247 app and join that way.
Can I put my membership on hold or freeze it?
Yes. Membership holds are available for up to 3 months (or longer in certain circumstances) for a $10/month hold fee.
Is there an enrollment or startup fee?
Yes. There is a $50 enrollment fee when you join. There is also an annual enhancement fee that helps us continue improving the gym, equipment, and overall member experience.
Does inFIT24 offer a day pass?
Yes. We offer a $10 day pass for a single visit, as well as week and month passes for people just in town for a short period.
Can I bring a guest with me?
Yes. Guests are welcome. They simply sign in at the front desk and pay a $10 guest pass fee. Additional guest policies are available at the front desk.
Try Before You Join
Can I try inFIT24 before committing to a membership?
Yes! Try inFIT24 with our $10 7-day trial membership, which includes 24/7 gym access, 1 sauna session, 1 group exercise class, and 1 group training session. It's a great way to experience everything inFIT has to offer before choosing the membership that's the best fit for you. Download the inFIT247 app to get started.
Discounts & Insurance
Does inFIT24 offer a military or first responder discount?
Yes. We offer a 10% Hero discount for military, police, and medical professionals. Contact us if you qualify.
Do you offer student or senior discounts?
We don't offer standard student or senior discounts, but we do offer short-term student memberships for students home from school during breaks and summer. We also work with Renew Active and SilverSneakers insurance programs that may benefit seniors.
What insurance and wellness programs does inFIT24 accept?
inFIT24 works with several wellness programs that may allow eligible members to join at reduced or no cost depending on their plan:

SilverSneakers® · Renew Active® · Active & Fit Standard · Active & Fit Premium · One Pass · Prime Fitness Your Way · Husk

Contact us to get signed up through one of these programs.
How do I use my SilverSneakers or Renew Active benefit at inFIT24?
Reach out to us and we'll get you signed up with your benefit. Contact us here or call/text 763-424-4435.
Access & Hours
Is inFIT24 open 24 hours a day?
Yes. Members have 24/7 access to the gym every single day of the year. Staff is available during designated hours — you can also reach us anytime at info@infit.org.
Is inFIT24 open on holidays?
Yes! You can work out any day you want with a membership — 365 days a year.
How do I access the gym after staffed hours?
Members use the inFIT247 app for secure 24/7 access to the gym, even when staff aren't on-site.
What app do I use to get into inFIT24?
inFIT24 has its own custom app. Search inFIT247 in the App Store or Google Play to download it. The app lets you access the gym, book sauna sessions, sign up for classes, and manage your membership.
What are the staffed hours at inFIT24?
Monday – Friday: 9:00 am – 1:00 pm
Monday – Thursday: 3:00 pm – 7:00 pm
Saturday: 8:00 am – 12:00 pm
Sunday: By appointment

The gym is open 24/7/365 — staff hours are just when our team is on-site to help.
Sauna
Does inFIT24 have an infrared sauna?
Yes. inFIT24 has a Sunlighten infrared sauna included with all membership options.
How do I book a sauna session?
Book and reserve your sauna spot directly in the inFIT247 app.
How long are sauna sessions?
You can reserve the sauna for a 30-minute or 60-minute time slot.
Is the sauna included with my membership?
Yes. Sauna sessions are included in all inFIT24 membership options.
Does inFIT24 provide towels for the sauna?
Yes. Towels are provided for sauna use. Please bring your own towel for showering or general gym use.
Classes & Group Training
What group fitness classes does inFIT24 offer?
inFIT24 is known for its wide variety of group exercise classes — from Spin, Boxing, and Cardio Circuit to Group Strength, Butts & Guts, Kettlebell, and more. With so many options, you'll always have something new to try!
How do I sign up for a class?
Sign up for classes directly in the inFIT247 app or in the member portal online. You can view your registrations and cancel right there too.
How much does it cost to drop in for a class?
You don't need a membership to take an inFIT class. Group Exercise classes are $15/session and Group Training sessions are $25/session for non-members. 5-packs are available to save. You can check yourself in at the door up to 15 minutes before class starts.
Does inFIT24 offer livestream classes?
Yes. Select classes are available via livestream through Zoom. Livestream classes are included with Premium and Ultimate memberships, or can be purchased individually so you can join from anywhere.
What is group training and how is it different from group fitness classes?
Group Training is led by a more highly certified personal trainer who focuses entirely on coaching, correcting form, and supporting you throughout the session. Group Exercise instructors are also certified, but specifically in group exercise, and lead the workout while participating alongside you. You can get a great workout with either — or both. The primary difference is the level of individual coaching and attention you receive.
Personal Training
Does inFIT24 offer personal training?
Yes. inFIT24 has several great personal trainers available to help you reach your fitness goals.
How do I get matched with a personal trainer?
Our team can help match you with a trainer based on your goals, experience, and preferences. Reach out to Mark@infit.org to get additional information and get connected.
Does inFIT24 offer youth or team training programs?
Yes. We offer individual, small-group, and team training for youth athletes. Workouts can be customized to fit your group's specific goals, age, fitness level, or sport.
General
Where is inFIT24 located?
inFIT24 is conveniently located in Albertville, MN just behind the Outlet Mall at 6589 Laketowne Place NE, Albertville, MN 55301. Get directions →
Is inFIT24 a good gym for beginners?
Absolutely. Whether you're just getting started or training for competition, inFIT has the equipment, coaching, and supportive community to help you reach your goals. Every Gym membership includes a free equipment orientation with a personal trainer.
Does inFIT24 have an on-site physical therapist?
Yes. Beth at Elevate Physical Therapy is dedicated to helping individuals achieve optimal functional movement and pain relief. Through comprehensive evaluations, tailored exercise programs, manual therapy, and patient education, she focuses on improving mobility and managing pain effectively. Visit Elevate PT →
Are there age requirements to use the gym?
Yes. Members must be 13 or older to use the gym. Members under 16 have limited access and must work out during staffed hours or with a family member who is a member (there is a separate check-in procedure for under-16 members). Youth fitness programming is available at select times. If you have a child under 13, please reach out to see if they may be a good fit for one of our youth programs.
Does inFIT24 provide towels?
Towels are provided for sauna use. Please bring your own towel for showering or general gym use. Gym wipes are provided to clean equipment after use.
How do I contact inFIT24?
You can stop in anytime during staffed hours for a tour or to get questions answered. You can also call or text us at 763-424-4435, email info@infit.org, or send a message through our website. We'd love to help you get started.
