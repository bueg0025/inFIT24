Common Questions

24 Hour Gym FAQ

Is inFIT24 really open 24 hours?

Yes — 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year. That includes Christmas, New Year's, and every other holiday. Members use the inFIT247 app to unlock the door at any hour without needing staff present.

How do I get in after hours?

Download the inFIT247 app (available on iOS and Android), create your member account, and use the app to check in and unlock the front door at any time. It works the same at 3am as it does at noon.

Is there a 24 hour gym near St. Michael, Otsego, or Rogers?

inFIT24 in Albertville is the closest 24/7 gym to St. Michael, Otsego, Rogers, Monticello, and Big Lake. We're located at 6589 Laketowne Place NE, just off I-94.

Do I need a contract to join?

No. All inFIT24 memberships are month-to-month. No annual commitment, no cancellation fees. Start when you're ready and stop whenever you choose.

What's included in a 24 hour gym membership?

All memberships include 24/7 facility access, the infrared sauna, cardio and strength equipment, free weights, functional training space, and private bathrooms with showers. Group classes and personal training are available as add-ons or in higher membership tiers.
